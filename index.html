PROCESS / 01

01 — IMPREGNATION LINE

Décor paper
impregnation.

To attain attractive and durable surfaces, furniture and flooring particleboards and MDF are usually thermo-fused with printed or solid-colour paper. This décor paper is saturated with melamine and urea resins in specially designed impregnation lines.

Discuss your requirements
02

Impregnation resins

Décor-paper impregnation resins consist of melamine-formaldehyde (MF) or urea-formaldehyde (UF) pre-polymers in aqueous solution.

03

Additives within impregnation lines

Impregnation resins require chemical additives and catalysts (hardener) to help saturate the core and coat the surface of décor paper.

04

Lamination of chipboard & MDF

Impregnated décor paper is laminated using shortcycle (LPL) or HPL processes on MDF, CB, Formica and Bakelite.
